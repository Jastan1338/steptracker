Healthy step guidelines

Most health bodies (like the WHO) frame activity guidance as about 150 minutes of moderate movement a week rather than a fixed step count — 10,000 steps is a popular benchmark, not an official medical target. These step ranges are commonly used general reference points:

SedentaryUnder 5,000
Low active5,000–7,499
Somewhat active7,500–9,999
Active10,000–12,499
Highly active12,500+

General information, not personalized medical advice — talk to a healthcare professional about what's right for you.
